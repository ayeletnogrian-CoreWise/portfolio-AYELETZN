Who Am I?

תיק העבודות הזה נבנה מתוך הגדלת ראש וזיהוי פער אמיתי: הבנתי שכדי להמחיש את היכולות שלי בניהול פרויקטים, People Operations ו־L&D, קורות חיים בלבד לא מספיקים. כשלא היה לי תיק עבודות שמציג את היכולות האלו בצורה מוחשית, החלטתי לבנות אותו בעצמי מאפס — ללמוד, לדייק, להתנסות וליצור תוצרים שמראים איך אני חושבת ופועלת בפועל.

בתהליך הבנייה לקחתי נושאים שלמדתי ופיתחתי במסגרת התואר והפכתי אותם לפרויקטים יישומיים: זיהוי אתגר ארגוני, בניית פתרון, תיעוד תהליך, יצירת דשבורדים, פיתוח תוצרים דיגיטליים וחיבור בין אסטרטגיה לביצוע. במהלך הדרך למדתי והשתמשתי בכלים כמו Excel, Notion, Figma, Canva, VS Code, GitHub וכלי AI כדי להפוך רעיונות לתוצרים מקצועיים ומוכנים ליישום.

עם תואר שני בפיתוח ושינוי ארגוני והתמחות בניהול גלובלי, לצד ניסיון בניהול צוות גיוס, הקמת מערכי הדרכה, הובלת תהליכים מבצעיים ועבודה חוצת ממשקים, אני מביאה איתי שילוב של חשיבה ארגונית, יכולת ביצוע גבוהה, למידה עצמאית ו־Ownership אמיתי על תהליכים מקצה לקצה.

Classification

Conceptual project based on academic research (M.A.)

Organizational Challenge

"Invisible friction" in distributed teams due to cultural gaps and asynchronous communication, harming productivity and psychological safety.

Business Value

Reduces friction in global teams by giving managers a clear communication framework, team agreements and measurement tools.

My Solution

Translated academic research into a practical toolkit (CLEAR model) adapted for multicultural work. Includes communication style mapping, Team Working Agreements (TWA), and a measurement dashboard for managers.

My Perspective

"As someone fascinated by cultures, it was important for me to build a managerial bridge — a framework that gives legitimacy to every culture while creating clear rules of engagement for synchronization. The goal was to turn cultural diversity from a friction source into an organizational asset."

Tools & Deliverables

  • Excel — Conflict Management Dashboard, resolution time tracking, psychological safety measurement
  • VS Code — Digital enablement portal architecture
  • Notion — Checklists, risk log, team working templates
  • Figma & Canva — Mediation tools & global manager communication guide

Organizational Challenge

AI adoption is first and foremost change management and barrier removal. My managerial angle was Enablement: not settling for training, but creating an infrastructure that gives employees the confidence to act independently and professionally.

Classification

Conceptual project based on academic research (M.A.)

Organizational Challenge

Inconsistency in Customer Success Manager training, creating professionalism gaps and a long, uneven ramp-up time.

Business Value

Shortens onboarding ramp-up by creating one structured source of truth for new CSMs and their managers.

My Solution

Redesigned the onboarding experience as a structured People Operations project. Built a gradual 90-day learning track including simulations, readiness assessment tools, and a central learning portal.

My Perspective

Good onboarding is the key to employee retention. As a manager who believes in "human command," it was important to me to create a framework that gives employees a soft landing and confidence, and gives managers the logistical peace of mind and the right tools for mentoring.

Tools & Deliverables

  • Notion — Central learning portal (Learning Hub) for managing the onboarding journey
  • Excel — Readiness tracking dashboard and ramp-up time measurement
  • PowerPoint — Training design for onboarding managers
  • Figma & Canva — Visual assets and simulations for the learning process

Proposed Pilot Measurement Framework

Key measurement areas: Ramp-up Time · Completion Rate · Repeat Questions

Classification

Conceptual project based on academic research (M.A.)

Organizational Challenge

Difficulty translating new business strategy into behavioral change on the ground. The strategy was announced, but adoption among middle management and employees stalled.

Business Value

Bridges the gap between executive strategy and employee behavior through role-based enablement and manager tools.

My Solution

Built a 5-week "Enablement" program for change management. Created impact maps by role, a manager conversation guide, and a knowledge portal centralizing all tools needed for a smooth transition.

My Perspective

Leading change requires bridging the gap between "management announcements" and the employee's daily reality. My angle was taking full ownership (Ownership) of the process — I built the infrastructure myself to ensure managers would have one clear "source of truth."

Tools & Deliverables

  • VS Code — Digital knowledge portal for rapid rollout
  • Excel — Readiness measurement dashboard and skill gap analysis
  • Figma — Role Impact Maps design and UI
  • Notion — Roadmap management and stakeholder synchronization

Proposed Pilot Measurement Framework
